📜 FEE is SOKOBA — INSTRUCTIONS

🎯 Objective: Move the green "FEE" character and push all the ★ Golden Crates onto the ⭐ Glowing Stars.

🕹️ Controls:
• Click on-screen arrow buttons OR use Keyboard Arrow Keys (↑ ↓ ← →).
• Push crates by walking into them — they slide one tile forward if empty.
• Crates cannot be pulled, and cannot pass through walls or other crates.
• Once all crates are on stars, you automatically advance to next level!

🌟 Pro Tips:
• Reset button restarts current level.
• Watch your moves counter — fewer moves = master solver!
• Some puzzles require careful order — plan ahead.
